2. College Location (iframe)

Purpose of using an iframe

  • An <iframe> (inline frame) shows another webpage inside the current page.
  • Here it embeds Google Maps, so visitors can see the college location without leaving our site.
  • It saves effort — we reuse a ready service instead of building our own map.
  • It is also used for YouTube videos, forms and documents.
